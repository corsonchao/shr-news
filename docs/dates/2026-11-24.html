Yukon Government Wants to Add Geothermal to Power Portfolio

Canada's Yukon territory is developing rules for deep geothermal energy, which is heat extracted from beneath the Earth's surface. The government wants to add this reliable, round-the-clock power source to its energy mix to reduce strain on its electrical grid, especially during brutal winters when demand nearly exceeded capacity. While small-scale ground heating systems exist, the territory has no utility-scale geothermal plants yet, though researchers believe the resource is abundant in the southwestern and south-central regions.
