▲ Eavor withdraws drilling rigs from the Geretsried geothermal project site in Germany

Eavor Technologies is temporarily removing its drilling equipment from its commercial geothermal project in Geretsried, Germany to reduce costs while it works on improving the project's business arrangements. The project, which uses Eavor's closed-loop geothermal technology (a system that circulates fluid through underground rock to extract heat without requiring traditional water reservoirs), started producing electricity but fell significantly short of expectations, generating only 0.5 to 2.0 megawatts instead of the planned output. The company plans to resume drilling for a second loop once it secures better commercial agreements with local heating partners.
