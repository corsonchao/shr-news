▲ Fervo Energy sets new drilling record with 5900m geothermal well at Cape Station, Utah

Fervo Energy drilled a geothermal well (a hole into the earth to access hot rock for energy) in Utah faster than ever before, completing it in just 21 days. This well, called Sawtooth 7, is deeper and hotter than previous versions and reaches nearly 6 kilometers underground with a long horizontal section. The company is using improved drilling techniques and well designs to access superhot rock more efficiently, which should make geothermal power plants cheaper and more productive.

▲ Kaishan breaks ground on turbine manufacturing facility in Loxley, Alabama

A Chinese company called Kaishan is building a factory in Alabama to manufacture turbines and other equipment used in geothermal power plants—systems that generate electricity from heat deep underground. The factory, expected to open by late 2027, will be the first geothermal equipment manufacturing facility in the US and will help meet growing demand for geothermal power as companies like data centers need more electricity.
