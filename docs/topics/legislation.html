▲ Study finds geothermal learning remains highly local

A new study analyzing 514 geothermal power plants over 70 years found that costs have actually increased rather than decreased as more geothermal capacity was built globally, unlike solar and wind which get cheaper with scale. The research shows that geothermal knowledge (understanding of underground water systems, drilling techniques, and local regulations) stays within specific regions and doesn't transfer easily between countries, because each location has unique geology and regulatory requirements. The study suggests that successful geothermal markets need continuous project pipelines and stable policies to retain specialized expertise, rather than relying on occasional large projects.
