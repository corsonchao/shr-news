Exploration permit granted for Barranquero geothermal site in Colombia

Colombia's government has approved a five-year exploration permit for the El Barranquero geothermal site, which will allow companies to drill deep wells, study underground rock formations, and assess the area's potential for generating heat energy from the earth. The project is expected to cost approximately $11.8 million and is part of the San Diego geothermal region, which experts estimate could produce 133.79 MW (a measure of electrical power output). This is the third major geothermal exploration project approved in Colombia recently, showing growing interest in this renewable energy source.
