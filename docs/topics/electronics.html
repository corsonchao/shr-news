▲ NLR highlights licensable technologies for deeper, more profitable geothermal drilling

The National Laboratory of the Rockies has identified three ready-to-use technologies that could make geothermal drilling deeper and more cost-effective. These include a high-temperature power generator that works in extreme heat, specialized sensors and electronics that collect drilling data in harsh conditions, and software that predicts costs for new geothermal projects. These innovations are already available for geothermal companies to license and use.
