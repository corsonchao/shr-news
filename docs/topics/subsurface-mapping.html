▲ CeraPhi Energy to evaluate geothermal repurposing of Star Energy oil and gas wells in the UK

CeraPhi Energy and Star Energy have partnered to convert old oil and gas wells in the UK into geothermal energy systems that produce renewable heat. CeraPhi will use its proprietary CeraPhiWell technology, which circulates special fluid down existing wells to extract heat from hot rock below ground, creating a closed system that can operate for at least 50 years. The deal targets up to 25 wells and represents a way to reuse existing infrastructure rather than drilling new wells, reducing costs and environmental impact of geothermal development.

Exploration permit granted for Barranquero geothermal site in Colombia

Colombia's government has approved a five-year exploration permit for the El Barranquero geothermal site, which will allow companies to drill deep wells, study underground rock formations, and assess the area's potential for generating heat energy from the earth. The project is expected to cost approximately $11.8 million and is part of the San Diego geothermal region, which experts estimate could produce 133.79 MW (a measure of electrical power output). This is the third major geothermal exploration project approved in Colombia recently, showing growing interest in this renewable energy source.

These projects are testing whether EGS technology can reliably produce electricity from superhot rock formations where natural geothermal conditions don't exist.
