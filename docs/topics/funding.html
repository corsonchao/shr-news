▲ Quaise Energy closes $134m Series B funding round to support superhot geothermal project

Quaise Energy raised $134 million in funding to develop Project Obsidian, a superhot geothermal power plant in Oregon that taps into extremely hot rock deep underground. The company uses a specialized millimeter wave drilling technology (a focused energy beam that can break apart rock) to drill into rocks at temperatures between 300 to 500°C, allowing them to build geothermal plants that produce as much power as fossil fuel plants. The first phase aims to generate 50 megawatts of electricity by 2030, with plans to expand to 250 megawatts.
