▲ Can Heat-as-a-Service unlock geothermal scale?

Heat-as-a-Service is a financing model where an investor owns and operates geothermal heating infrastructure while customers pay for heat through long-term contracts, removing the need for customers to fund expensive upfront costs. This approach can make geothermal more accessible to businesses and buildings, though larger utilities may prefer to maintain ownership and control of their heating systems. Geothermal heating projects can attract conventional loans and investment once their risks are clearly identified and managed, rather than eliminated entirely.
