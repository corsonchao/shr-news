Currently, conventional geothermal wells reach temperatures of 250-350°C and produce 2-7 megawatts of power, but this new project targets depths of 4-5 kilometers where temperatures exceed 400°C, with the goal of producing 20-50 megawatts per well. The company plans a four-stage testing program starting in late 2026 to prove the concept works and to develop materials and equipment that can safely handle these extreme underground conditions.
